Fathom

One event, followed down every layer of a machine, on one clock. The machine is a three-stage RV32I core (lowRISC Ibex), synthesised to gates, mapped to Sky130 cells, placed and routed. It is not the computer in front of you; every layer is a model of that core and says so in its header.

  • ←→ step a cycle · ⇧ ×10 · HomeEnd
  • ↓ descend into the next layer · ↑ ascend · Esc all layers
  • click a source line, an assembly row, a pipeline column, a gate bar, or a layer's header
  • the bottom two layers load on demand (DuckDB-wasm, 18 MB, same-origin)
  • agents: window.fathom — describe · seek · descend · ascend · stateAt · trace · loadBottom · history

version · every artifact is precomputed and read-only · no network beyond this origin · ? or Esc closes
